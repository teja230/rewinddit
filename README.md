# Rewinddittt

Rewinddittt is your daily Reddit time-travel game.

Every day, you get 5 memorable moments from Reddit history with the obvious clues hidden. Your challenge is to guess the year each one happened.

When you submit your guesses, you instantly see:
- The real year
- A short reveal about what happened
- Your points for each question

## Daily Fun, Fast to Play

- 5 quick questions each day
- Simple slider-based guessing
- Daily score out of 500
- Streak tracking for consecutive days played
- Daily and all-time leaderboards

## Built for Redditors

Some moments are legendary. Some are chaotic. Some are pure internet history.

Whether you have been on Reddit for years or just joined, Rewinddittt is made to be fun, surprising, and easy to jump into.

## See You Tomorrow

A new challenge appears every day, so come back, protect your streak, and climb the leaderboard.
